# SRVCABLE — Sarvottam Cables Ltd
As of: 2026-09-23

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) makes power cables, wires and conductors; Q1 FY27 ended on 30 June 2026. [S-e4dc6442b586]
Q1 consolidated revenue rose 18.0% to ₹1,248 crore per the issuer, conflicting with Business Daily's report of ₹1,428 crore and 35% growth. [S-e4dc6442b586] [S-a41e9e2f21c8]
Profit after tax rose 5.9% to ₹82 crore. EBITDA, an operating profitability measure, fell as a share of revenue from 12.9% to 11.2%. [S-e4dc6442b586]

## Bull case

The order book reached ₹3,900 crore at 30 June 2026, versus ₹3,150 crore a year earlier; exports contributed 21% of Q1 FY27 revenue, versus 16% in Q1 FY26. [S-e4dc6442b586]

In August, management maintained 15–17% FY27 revenue growth guidance and expected Bharuch commercial production in Q3 FY27, adding about 30% cable capacity. [S-3a36f4ac4109] [S-e4dc6442b586]

On the August call, management said data-centre cables represented about 9% of domestic revenue and thought that share could double in two years. [S-3a36f4ac4109]

Pledges were 4.1% of promoter holdings at 30 June 2026, versus 11.5% a year earlier; the 35% news report was published in March 2024. [S-32ec5735c356] [S-156eefbe9bcf]

## Bear case

On the August call, management attributed margin pressure mainly to copper; about two-thirds of contracts permit price changes, with resets delayed by one to two quarters. [S-3a36f4ac4109]

The CFO reported receivable days increasing from 78 to 96 over the prior year, mainly because two state utilities paid slowly; no defaults had been observed. [S-3a36f4ac4109]

The CFO put total Bharuch capital expenditure at ₹620 crore, about 70% already spent, funded mainly with internally generated cash and some loans; net debt was 0.35 times equity. [S-3a36f4ac4109]

On 2 September, the company disclosed a ₹46.3 crore GST demand, including interest and penalty, relating to FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 input tax credits. [S-4a0ebad90ad1]

The company intended to appeal and expected no material financial or operational impact. [S-4a0ebad90ad1]
